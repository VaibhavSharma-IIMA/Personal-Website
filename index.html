A life of crossroads

In 2018 I wrote the NDA exam and got All India Rank 10, which meant I could have joined the Indian Air Force as a pilot. I picked engineering instead, because building things was what I really enjoyed, though the discipline that exam asked for has stayed with me.

At NSUT Delhi I joined the satellite team in my first year, and by my second year I was leading it. There were ten of us, and we finished second in the world at CanSat 2021, a competition sponsored by NASA. After college I spent two years as a software engineer at LinkedIn. There was a lot to learn about how a big company works, and I ended up building platforms that teams across LinkedIn used.

I had deferred my IIM Ahmedabad admit for those two years and joined in 2025. The finance courses in first year made me want to see how deals actually get done, so I spent the summer at Deutsche Bank as an investment banking intern, learning how to value a business and check whether the numbers hold up. Now I'm an Investment Partner at Campus Fund, where I back student founders.

TEAM Observability Engineering · Logs & Events, LinkedIn Bangalore. We owned two company-wide platforms: the centralized logging platform and the events store. Logs were collected by Fluent Bit agents running on LinkedIn's servers, streamed through Kafka, and landed in Azure Data Explorer, where engineers searched them with KQL. Events came in through emitter libraries (Python and Go) and an API layer, and flowed through Kafka into the events store. Our own services were written mostly in Go and Python, with MySQL and Couchbase behind internal tools like the traffic-shift tool.

What I built on

Logs

Our team ran the central logs platform, which takes in 9PB+ of logs a day and keeps them searchable in real time. I moved 3PB+ of that daily ingestion across Azure regions on my own, which saves $1M+ a year.

  • ingestion
  • migration
  • alerting
  • cost

Events

With my team, I helped design the central events platform from scratch, picking features based on how teams actually sent events. I built the Python emitter library and the API layer, then worked with 5+ producer teams to get them onboarded.

  • architecture
  • SDKs
  • APIs
  • onboarding

Reliability

During incidents, LinkedIn moves user traffic between datacenters with a traffic-shift tool. I made those shifts 83% faster and cut the tool's data-loss window to a day by moving its database from Couchbase to MySQL. On call, I handled 20+ critical incidents.

  • on-call
