A life of crossroads

In 2018 I sat the NDA exam with no coaching and no prep, just to see how I'd do, and came out with All India Rank 10. That opened the door to life as a pilot in the Indian Air Force. It was the first real fork in my road, and I chose engineering, the thing I actually loved. I kept the discipline and grit the exam had asked of me.

At NSUT Delhi I walked into a satellite team knowing nothing about satellites. Two years later, as a sophomore, I was leading ten people to International Rank 2 at NASA-sponsored CanSat. At LinkedIn I didn't know how the corporate world worked either. I learned fast and ended up building platforms the whole company relied on.

Then the next fork: I deferred my IIM Ahmedabad admit for two years and finally took it up in 2025. First-year finance courses pulled me towards markets, a summer at Deutsche Bank taught me how to underwrite a business rather than just admire it, and today I back the people doing the building.

Outlearn, outwork, outpersevere. Curious, disciplined and collaborative: someone who takes on challenges to grow, and to build something meaningful with others.

TEAM Observability Engineering · Logs & Events, LinkedIn Bangalore. We owned two company-wide platforms: the centralized logging platform and the events store. Logs were collected by Fluent Bit agents running on LinkedIn's servers, streamed through Kafka, and landed in Azure Data Explorer, where engineers searched them with KQL. Events came in through emitter libraries (Python and Go) and an API layer, and flowed through Kafka into the events store. Our own services were written mostly in Go and Python, with MySQL and Couchbase behind internal tools like the traffic-shift tool.
